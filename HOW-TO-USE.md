# How to Use the 55Places Content Dashboard

Welcome, Deana and Kelly! This dashboard is your one-stop shop for all the 55Places social content we've prepared. Everything you need to post, build in Canva, or reference is right here. Here's how to use it.

---

## 1. Open the Dashboard

Open the link you were given (it looks like clients.growthlever.app/55places). It works on your phone too — just bookmark it. No login needed.

## 2. Understand What You're Looking At

The top of the page shows summary stats — how many posts are ready, how many platforms are covered, and how many personas we're speaking to. Think of this as your content studio control panel.

## 3. Use the Navigation Tabs

The tab bar just below the header is how you move between sections. You'll see: Overview, LinkedIn, Instagram, Facebook Ads, Reddit, Video/TikTok, Calendar, and Files. Click any tab to jump to that section. On mobile, you can scroll the tabs left and right.

## 4. Start with the Overview Tab

This is your home base. It shows the three audience personas (Linda, Diane, and Robert & Carol), the top 5 blog posts we selected, and a count of every content type. Read the persona cards first — knowing who you're talking to makes everything else click.

## 5. Browse Posts by Platform

Click the LinkedIn, Instagram, Reddit, or Facebook Ads tab to see every post for that platform. Each post is displayed in a card that looks like how it would appear on the actual platform.

## 6. Read a Post Card

Every post card shows:
- The post title and which persona it's for (color-coded tag)
- The topic it covers
- The full post text inside a platform-style frame
- Any hashtags (for Instagram posts)
- Two buttons at the bottom: Edit and Share

## 7. Copy a Post to Use It

Click the blue **Share** button on any card. A window pops up with the full post text and a "Copy to Clipboard" button. Click it, and the text is copied. Then open the platform (LinkedIn, Instagram, etc.) and paste it right in.

You'll see a quick confirmation message at the bottom of the screen when the copy works.

## 8. Find the Source File to Make Edits

Click the **Edit** button on any card. It shows you the file path where the original content lives (like `/55places/social-copy/linkedin-pros-cons-linda.md`). Open that file on your computer to edit the text directly. Any changes you make there will be the new version.

## 9. Use the Calendar

Click the **Calendar** tab to see the 30-day posting schedule. It's a grid with 4 weeks and 7 days. Each day shows colored chips representing scheduled posts:
- Blue = LinkedIn
- Pink = Instagram
- Dark blue = Facebook
- Orange = Reddit

Hover over any chip to see the full post name and persona.

## 10. Check the Files Tab

The Files tab shows every file in the project, organized by category (Personas, Social Copy, Ad Copy, Video Scripts, Visual Briefs). Each file has a short description of what's inside so you know exactly where to find things.

## 11. Build Videos

Click the Video/TikTok tab to see the 5 video scripts. Each shows the recommended format (Slideshow, Voiceover, or Talking-Head), the tool to use (CapCut, HeyGen, or Runway), and a preview of the script. Click "Copy Script" to get the full text.

For detailed production sheets with timecodes, open the files in the `/55places/video-production/` folder.

## 12. What About Direct Posting?

For now, this dashboard is for viewing, copying, and organizing your content. Direct posting to social platforms is coming in a future update. For now, the copy-and-paste workflow is quick and keeps you in control of what goes live.

## 13. Something Look Wrong?

If any content looks off, a link doesn't work, or you have questions about what to post when, reach out to your GrowthLever team. We're here to help.

---

You're about to save a ton of time. Everything is ready — just open, copy, and post. Have fun with it!
